Writing

Birthday card, people, and marks

Unit 1 · Which birthday card is right?

Dear is who gets the card. From is who writes it. Names start with a capital letter.

Write your own card

Use an English name. The first letter is a capital, like Lily or Ken.

  • The friend's name starts with a capital
  • Your name starts with a capital
  • It has Dear, Happy birthday!, and From

Unit 2 · Introduce people at school

A sentence starts with a capital. A name starts with a capital. A sentence ends with a full stop. These four lines can be read aloud.

My name is Amy.

I am six years old.

This is Tom.

He is my classmate.

Unit 4 · What does the comic say?

You are talking to Super Boy. Put a comma before the name. A shout can use an exclamation mark.
