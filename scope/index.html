Unit 1 Getting to know you

Names, ages, and birthday cards

On the first day, Jack Poon, Lily Wong, and Ken Chan meet in class. Jack is seven. Today is his birthday. Lily and Ken are six. They say Good morning and Nice to meet you. They ask names and ages. Lily opens her lunch box, takes out a cake, and says Happy birthday.

🌅 morning☀️ afternoon👋 Good morning😊 Good afternoon🙂 Hello✋ Hi🏷️ name🎂 birthday
  • Ask a name: What is your name? My name is Lily.
  • Spell a name: How do you spell your name? L-I-L-Y.
  • Ask an age: How old are you? I am six years old.
  • Say hello: Nice to meet you.

Unit 4 In my school bag

Things in the bag

Super Boy walks to school on a sunny morning. He has an eraser and a school bag. He helps a cat and a child. A grandma feels unwell. Super Boy has eight pencils and two water bottles. He gives her a bottle of water. She says Thank you. He says No problem.

  • I have and he has: I have a pen. He has an eraser. She has three pencils.
  • a and an: a book, an eraser
  • Numbers 1 to 10: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten

How the paper usually asks

  • Listening: circle the word you hear, or pick the sound.
  • Choice: after a short story, pick the best answer.
  • Fill the gap: This is ___. He is our ___.
  • Phonics: hear a word and circle h / m / y, b / g / t, or p / k / -en.
  • Grammar: I am / He is / She is / We are; I have / He has; a / an.
  • Writing: a birthday card, a person sentence with capitals and a full stop, and a speech bubble.

Rhyme order

Unit 4 asks you to put the pictures in rhyme order, 1 to 5. The last word of each line rhymes.

  1. 1. One, two, take out your glue.
  2. 2. Three, four, touch the floor.
  3. 3. Five, six, write some ticks.
  4. 4. Seven, eight, sit up straight.
  5. 5. Nine, ten, put down your pen.
