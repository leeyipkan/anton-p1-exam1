Patterns

Ask a name

What is your name? My name is Lily.

Ask the name, then say My name is ...

  • My name is Jack Poon.
  • My name is Ken Chan.

Spell a name

How do you spell your name? L-I-L-Y.

Say one letter at a time.

  • J-A-C-K
  • K-E-N

Ask an age

How old are you? I am six years old.

Ask the age, then say I am ... years old.

  • I am seven years old.
  • I am six years old too.

Say hello

Nice to meet you.

Say this when you meet a new friend.

  • Hi, Lily. Nice to meet you.
  • You are welcome.
